--------------------------------------------------

Definition

Profit is the excess of Revenue over Expenses during an accounting period.

--------------------------------------------------

Formula

Profit = Revenue – Expenses

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

Suppose

Sales = ₹10,00,000

Expenses = ₹8,00,000

Profit = ₹2,00,000

--------------------------------------------------

Importance

Profit shows the success of a business.

It allows expansion, investment and future growth.

--------------------------------------------------

Types

Gross Profit

Net Profit

Operating Profit

--------------------------------------------------

Teacher Tip

Profit is the reward for taking business risk.

--------------------------------------------------

Common Mistakes

❌ High Sales always mean High Profit.

✔ Profit depends upon both Revenue and Expenses.

--------------------------------------------------

Learning Outcome

Students calculate Profit using simple financial data.
