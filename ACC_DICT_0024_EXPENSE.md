--------------------------------------------------

Definition

An Expense is the cost incurred by a business to earn revenue.

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

A shop earns money by selling products.

To sell those products,

it pays

Rent

Salary

Electricity

Advertising

Transport

These payments are called Expenses.

--------------------------------------------------

Examples

Salary

Rent

Insurance

Telephone

Internet

Repairs

Wages

Fuel

--------------------------------------------------

Importance

Expenses reduce Profit.

Proper control of expenses improves business performance.

--------------------------------------------------

Formula

Profit = Revenue – Expenses

--------------------------------------------------

Teacher Tip

Ask students

"Can a business earn money without spending anything?"

--------------------------------------------------

Common Mistakes

❌ Buying Machinery is an Expense.

✔ Machinery is an Asset.

--------------------------------------------------

Learning Outcome

Students identify Revenue and Capital Expenses correctly.
