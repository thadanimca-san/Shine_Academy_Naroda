Partnership Deed

Definition

A Partnership Deed is a written agreement containing the rights, duties, responsibilities, and terms agreed upon by all partners.

Simple Explanation

Before starting a business together,

partners should decide

Who invests?

Who manages?

Who receives salary?

How will profit be shared?

All these decisions are written in the Partnership Deed.

Contents

• Name of Firm

• Name of Partners

• Capital Contribution

• Profit Sharing Ratio

• Salary

• Interest on Capital

• Interest on Drawings

• Admission Rules

• Retirement Rules

• Dissolution Rules

Importance

A good deed prevents future disputes.

Teacher Tip

Tell students:

"Friendship is based on trust.

Business requires trust plus documentation."

Learning Outcome

Students understand why written agreements are essential.
