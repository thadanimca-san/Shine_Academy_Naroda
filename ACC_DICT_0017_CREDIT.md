Definition

Credit is the right side of an account used to record increases or decreases according to accounting principles.

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

Every accounting account has two sides.

Left = Debit

Right = Credit

--------------------------------------------------

Golden Rules

Personal Account

Credit the Giver

Real Account

Credit What Goes Out

Nominal Account

Credit All Income and Gains

--------------------------------------------------

Examples

Capital Introduced

Capital A/c Cr.

Sales Made

Sales A/c Cr.

Cash Paid

Cash A/c Cr.

--------------------------------------------------

Common Mistake

Credit does NOT always mean decrease.

It depends upon the account.

--------------------------------------------------

Teacher Tip

Teach Debit and Credit together.

Never separately.

--------------------------------------------------

Learning Outcome

Students understand the Credit side of accounts.
