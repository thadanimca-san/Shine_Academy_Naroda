Definition

A Creditor is a person, business, or organization to whom the business owes money because goods or services have been purchased on credit.

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

Suppose ABC Traders purchase furniture worth ₹50,000 from XYZ Furniture House but promise to pay after one month.

Until payment is made, XYZ Furniture House is a Creditor of ABC Traders.

--------------------------------------------------

Examples

• Furniture Supplier

• Stationery Supplier

• Raw Material Supplier

• Wholesaler

• Machinery Supplier

--------------------------------------------------

Accounting Perspective

Creditors are Current Liabilities because payment is expected within the normal operating cycle.

--------------------------------------------------

Journal Entry

Purchase A/c Dr.

      To Creditor A/c

(Being goods purchased on credit.)

--------------------------------------------------

Balance Sheet Effect

Current Liabilities Increase

--------------------------------------------------

Importance

Businesses often purchase goods on credit to maintain cash flow.

Good relationships with creditors improve business operations.

--------------------------------------------------

Difference Between Debtor and Creditor

Debtor owes money to the business.

Creditor is owed money by the business.

--------------------------------------------------

Common Mistakes

❌ Creditors are Assets.

✔ Creditors are Liabilities.

--------------------------------------------------

Teacher Tip

Use the sentence:

"When we buy now and pay later, the seller becomes our Creditor."

--------------------------------------------------

Learning Outcome

Students will identify Creditors in journal entries and Balance Sheets.
