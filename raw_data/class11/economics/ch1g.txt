( 6 ) Discuss fully the definition of economics
given by Robbins.
Ans. According to Prof. Lionel Robbins,
'Economics is a science of scarcity.' The study
of an economic problem arising out of scarcity
of means is at the centre of study in Economics.
Nature has not provided enough resources or
means to satisfy all wants of all human beings.
Hence, man has to choose which means are to
be used to satisfy which of his wants. Economics
studies this scarcity aspect of resources.
➔ Robbins

defines Economics as a science of
scarcity, because ( 1) Wants are unlimited .
(2) Means or resources are scarce in relation to
wants. (3) The means are capable of alternative
uses.

➔ We

can derive following conclusions from
Robbins" definition :

( 1 ) Economics studies how man tries to
satisfy his wants with limited means. Thus, it is
a science of human behaviour also.

( 2 ) Economics studies not only physical goods
but non-physical services also because both satisfy
human wants.
( 3) Economics is neutral between ends, i.e .,
human wants. It does not consider whether a
particular want (e.g., drinking) is good or bad.
( 4 ) The basic problem in Economics is the
problem of choice. Man has to select his want
and then apply scarce means economically to
satisfy it.

15

( 5) Economics studies human behaviour. It
helps allocation or distribution of means in order to
satisfy human wants in such a way that maximum
satisfaction is attained. Man has to choose the
means and its quantity to achieve the given end
(i.e ., satisfaction of want). Thus, Economics is
sometimes called 'A science of choice.·
4 6 ) Economics

provides a principle for
economically using scarce means with alternative
uses in order to achieve maximum satisfaction.
Therefore it is sometimes called 'A science of
economising.' This kind of definition presented
by Robbins is widely accepted. However, certain
criticisms are levelled against Robbins' definition
also. They are as under :
( i ) If Economics has a role to play in
increasing social welfare and economic growth, it
has to · offer guidance for choosing what is good
for society while satisfying human wants.
( ii ) It is argued by critics that Robbins has

ignored macroeconomic aspects. It is pointed
out that it concentrates only on production and
distribution. This criticism is opposed by other
economists.
(iii) The scope of Economics has been
narrowed down by ignoring problems of economic
growth of a country. Some other economists argue
that Robbins' definition can be made applicable
to allocation of resources (i.e., means) such that
country can develop faster.

In spite of various criticisms made against
Robbins' definition, it is widely accepted as a
standard definition of Economic Science.
