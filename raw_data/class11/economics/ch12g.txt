When irrigate the soil and fill the space

gushes out. And as the interior becomes watery

die. Plant growth is stunted as leaves do not get
nutrients through the roots. Leaves turn yellow. It
is popular in chemical agriculture to apply urea

wrong. In fact the soil should be watered only
enough to allow water vapour to form, throught
the heat present in soil and excess water damages
the developmen t of crop. Similarly less water than
required is also harmful. Hence, only limited i.e.,
enough water should be supplied due to which
Whapasa is formed in the soil.

3. Straw Mulch : Straw mulching is done by
spreading dry woody material over the soil surface.
Straw mulching creates the necessary
environment, allowing earthworms and countless
millions of microorganisms to carry out their life
cycle 24 hours a day. Mulching also protects the
earthworms from etreme heat, cold and birds.
When the soil had the excess moisture, it absorbs
excess moisture from the soil and releases it
into the air. But when soil needs moisture, the

stores it in humus. In this way, 15 % irrigation
water is saved.
