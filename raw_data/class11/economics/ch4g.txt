* Schedule of Supply : A schedule showing a

* Relationship between Price and Supply : There
is positive relationship between price and supply,
because of the following reasons :
( 1 ) When price rises, sellers pursue the objective
of profit maximisation by supplying more
quantity at a higher price.
( 2) When price rises, possibility of profit
increases. Hence, new producers enter in the
market. As a result total supply increases.

* Exceptions to the Law of Supply : Exceptions
to law of supply arise when supply of a good
rises when its price falls and supply falls when
price rises, other factors are held unchanged.

Exceptions :
( 1 ) Rare goods namely ancient coins. ancient
handicrafts, ancient idols, old books, etc.
( 2 ) Perishable · goods like milk and milk
products, green vegetables, meat, eggs. fish.
ripe-fruits, flowers, etc.

* Price Determination in the Market :
• Price determination is the process of
determining a stable price at which goods
are bought and sold in the market.
• Prices are determined by the interaction of
market demand and supply.
• In a perfect competitive market. a price at
which market demand and market supply
become equal is the equilibrating process of
the market known as market equllibrium and
the price is called equilibrium price.
• Till there is no change in demand or supply
of goods, the equilibrium price remains in
the market.
