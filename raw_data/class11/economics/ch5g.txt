Objective
• To enable the student to be acquainted with different forms of market and to understand them.

* Meaning of a Market : Market is the system
through which the buyers and sellers get in
contact with each other directly or indirectly
for the sale or purchase of goods or services.

* Characteristics of a Market : In a market (1 l There
are numerous buyers and sellers. (2) There is
eXistence of goods and services. (3) The buyers
and sellers come in contact with each other.
(4) There is trend of equal price and (5 l The

buyers and sellers have information about the
market situation.

* Classification of Market :
( 1 ) Location based Market :
( i) Local Market: It is the market where
the products and services are produced
and sold at the same place. For
example, market for clay utensils,
market for the services of barber,
carpenter, tailor, etc.
