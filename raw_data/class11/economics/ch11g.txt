efforts make a person give up greed and
enable the person to get employment
and thus get freedom from poverty.
3. Use of Machinery : Gandhiji asserted
that machines are for human beings
and human beings are not for machines.
Hence machines must not be used so
widely that human beings become highly
dependent on machines.

3. An insight into what is attainable and

• Hence, considering labour production

4. Ownership of Assets :
• In a capitalist system, there is a
private ownership of wealth without
state control. In socialist system private
property has no place.
• Pandit Deendayal Upadhyay states
that abolition of private ownership
completely is unwise. Because it creates
loss of incentives and willingness to
work.
• Therefore, while people must be allowed
to own private wealth, limits must
be set on holding of property on the
basis of human values.
• He believed that uncontrolled possession
and self willed use of property destroys
the ethical and intellectual character
of a society.
• He s uggested setting up of law, social
governance and absolute decentralisation
of power in setting such limits.
• He believed that the use of assets
and wealth s hould be done for the
development of human beings.
5. Decentralised Economy:
• Centralisation of authority and assets
exists in capitalism and socialism
hinders human development.
• Pandit Deendayal Upadhyay suggested
a decentralised economic set up that
promotes overall development and
increases interaction among people.

• Decentralisation of power can be
attaine d by entrusting decisions
regarding production, distribution and
consumption to people.
• He believed that decentralisation of
economic power can help in solving
the major problems of Indian economy.
• He emphasised the development of
cottage and small-scale industries.
6. Restricting Consumption :
• Pandit Deendayal Upadhyay states
that our economic goal should be to
limit consumption keeping in mind
the development of the nation.
• He proposed to make the country
self-reliant by adopting the policy of
limiting consumption.
• Even developed capitalist nations are
also working towards applying the
concept of limited consumption to
resolve certain issues.
• According to Pandit Deendayal appropriate amount of production and
appropriate distribution can be made
possible by limiting consumption.
7. Labour Intensive Method of Production :
• Pandit Deendayal proposed labour
intensive methods of production for
India considering the labour abundance
and scarcity of capital in India.
• He suggested that to reduce the burden
of population on land , small industries
should be set up.
• He asserted that the motto of planning
should be 'work for all' and plans to
promote labour intensive method wWch
raise employment.
