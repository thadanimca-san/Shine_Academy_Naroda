* Meaning of National Income:
• Generally, the total monetary value of the total
production and services in a country during a
year is the National Income of that country.
• In other words, the total income received by
a country from economic activities during a
year is known as National Income.

* Definition of National Income :

( 1 ) Alfred Marshall :
• The net production of physical (tangible)
and non-physical (service) things by using
natural wealth (land) with capital and
labour in a country during the year is
the national income of the country.
• Prof. Marshall considered net production
of goods and services in his definition.
Therefore it is production oriented.
( 2 ) Irving Fisher :
• The proportion of direct consumption
of goods and services by the people of
a country during a year is called the
national income.
• Fisher considered consumption of goods
and services in his defmition. Therefore
it is consumption oriented.
( 3 ) A. C. Pigou :
• National income is a flow of those goods
and services whose payments have been
done through money or which can be
easily presented by money.
• In other words, the national income is the
total income of society along with foreign
income which can be easily measured by

money.
• Pigou considered money in his definition.
Therefore it is money oriented.

Ans. Since 1954, the Central Statistical

Certain problems arise while measuring national

1. Problem of double counting : Due to
double counting the estimate of national income
obtained is more. Therefore real national income
is not obtained. The problem of double counting
should be avoided.
2. Problem of self-consumption : Some goods
and services are for self-consumption and are
not sold in the market. Therefore they have no
monetary value. As a result we get low estimate
of the national income.
3. Problem of depreciation : Due to use of
factors of production they become Inefficient, which
is called depreciation and its exact value cannot
be obtained which results in not getting real
national income.

4. Problem of tax avoidance and tax evasion :
Due to tendency of tax avoidance and tax evasion.
the true income of businessmen cannot be obtained
and as a result estimate of real national income
cannot be obtained.
5. Problem of illegal income : Income
generated by way of gambling, robbery. black
money, smugling, etc. satisfy the need of persons
but it is not considered in national income.
Therefore real estimate of national income cannot
be obtained.
6. Problem of net foreign income : Due to
lack of complete and exact details of income
received by citizens of country in foreign and by
foreigners in our country, the net foreign income
cannot be calculated. Therefore real national income
cannot be known.
