➔ Increasing the role of private sector and market

oriented processes in economic activities of India
in place of regulated economic processes in India's
mixed economic system is called liberalisation.
➔ In the process of liberalisation there is a gradual

decline in the controls imposed by the state in
economic functioning, the role of market forces
of demand and supply increases, the private
sector is allowed to enter the investment for
public sector and protection granted by the
state to domestic industries against foreign
competition is reduced .
Economic changes in the process of Liberalisation:

( 1 ) MRTP Act was replaced by competition Act
in 2002 , which was aimed at reducing unhealthy
competition among enterprises.
( 2 ) FERA was replaced by FEMA in 1999
which aimed at managing foreign exchange earnings
and transactions of enterprises instead of regulating
those.
( 3 l As a change in industrial policy only three
sectors are kept reserved for the public sector
namely atomic energy. Some materials related to
atomic energy and railways.
( 4 ) Raising the investment limit for smallscale units so that they can adopt modernisation.
( 5 ) '.Automatic licensing route' was introduced
for investment by foreign companies in India.
( 6 ) Relaxation in export-import rules were
announced.
( 7 ) Foreign exchange was allowed to be
converted at market rates.
( 8 ) Expenditure on subsidies was attempted
to be reduced as part of the fiscal policy changes.
