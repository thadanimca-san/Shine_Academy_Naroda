[ Linear sketch of temples in Southern India]
1. Garbha Gruh: A small and dark rectangular

room where idol of deity is kept. It is known
as 'Garbharo' in Gujarat.
2. Gopuram : Gateway of temples in south India.

1\vo lower storeys are convex shaped and the
other storeys are afloat to make a pyramidical
structure that lends strength to the Gopuram.
3. Mandap : A huge hall constructed on the pillar

or a huge area lying in front of the main gate.
4. Shikhar : Pinnacle of the temple which is the
pointed exterior part of the Garbha Gruh. It
is brass / gold plated.

5. Vunan : A part of the temple that is semi-circular
or sloping in shape. It has many storeys and
looks like a pyramid.

[ Linear sketch of a mosque]
1. Galiyara : The way to enter and exit from the

mosque.
2. Kibla: A hall where namaz is performed. It is
always in the direction of Kaba.
3. Livan : The room in a mosque with pillars.

4. Maksura : End wall of the mosque , separated
by a railing.

5. Mahrab : The interior part of the mosque wall
which is of average human height and indicates
the correct direction of Mecca. In India Mahrab
is on the west side.
