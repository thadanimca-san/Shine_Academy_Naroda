( 5 ) Explain

th e concept of 'Sustainable

Ans. SuStatnable development has been defined
as a process of development wherein most of the
requirements of the present generation are met
witl1out compromising tl1e capability of the future
generations to meet their needs.

Sustainable development is advocated due to
the extreme adverse effects of human activities on
ecolog_y. If the present generation uses the natural
resources at high speed, the resources will get
depleted and nothing will remain for the future
generations. To protect the interests of the future
generations some •restraints have been suggested
in the strategy of sustainable development.
