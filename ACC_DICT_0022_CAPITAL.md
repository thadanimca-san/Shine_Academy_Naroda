Definition

Capital is the amount of money or the value of assets invested by the owner into the business for starting and operating the business.

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

Every business needs money to start.

The money invested by the owner is called Capital.

Without capital, a business cannot purchase furniture, machinery, stock, or equipment.

--------------------------------------------------

Real Life Example

Mr. Patel starts a grocery shop.

He invests

Cash ₹4,00,000

Furniture ₹80,000

Computer ₹40,000

Total Capital = ₹5,20,000

--------------------------------------------------

Types of Capital

• Fixed Capital

• Working Capital

• Owner's Capital

• Additional Capital

--------------------------------------------------

Accounting Effect

Capital increases the owner's claim on the business.

Importance

• Starts the business

--------------------------------------------------

Common Mistakes

❌ Capital means only cash.

✔ Capital may include cash, machinery, furniture, building and other assets invested by the owner.
