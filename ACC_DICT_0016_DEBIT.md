Definition

Debit is the left side of an account used to record increases or decreases according to accounting rules.

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

Every account has two sides.

Left Side = Debit

Right Side = Credit

--------------------------------------------------

Golden Rules

Personal Account

Debit the Receiver

Real Account

Debit What Comes In

Nominal Account

Debit All Expenses and Losses

--------------------------------------------------

Examples

Furniture Purchased

Furniture A/c Dr.

Salary Paid

Salary A/c Dr.

Cash Received

Cash A/c Dr.

--------------------------------------------------

Teacher Tip

Always draw a big "T" account while teaching Debit.

--------------------------------------------------

Common Mistake

Debit does NOT always mean increase.

It depends upon the type of account.

--------------------------------------------------

Learning Outcome

Students correctly identify Debit entries.
