Anneliese Marie ‘Anne’ Frank (12
June 1929 – February/March1945) was a German – bornJewish girl who wrote while inhiding with her family and fourfriends in Amsterdam during theGerman occupation of theNetherlands in World War II. Herfamily had moved to Amsterdamafter the Nazis gained power inGermany but were trapped whenthe Nazi occupation extended intothe Netherlands. As persecutionsagainst the Jewish populationincreased, the family went into
hiding in July 1942 in hidden rooms in her father Otto Frank’soffice building. After two years in hiding, the group was betrayedand transported to the concentration camp system where Anne diedof typhus in Bergen-Belsen within days of her sister, Margot Frank.Her father, Otto, the only survivor of the group, returned to Amsterdamafter the war ended, to find that her diary had been saved. Convincedthat it was a unique record, he took action to have it published inEnglish under the name The Diary of a Young Girl .
The diary was given to Anne Frank for her thirteenth birthday
and chronicles the events of her life from 12 June 1942 until itsfinal entry of 1 August 1944. It was eventually translated from itsoriginal Dutch into many languages and became one of the world’smost widely read books. There have also been several films,television and theatrical productions, and even an opera, based onthe diary. Described as the work of a mature and insightful mind,the diary provides an intimate examination of daily life under Nazioccupation. Anne Frank has become one of the most renowned anddiscussed of the Holocaust victims.
