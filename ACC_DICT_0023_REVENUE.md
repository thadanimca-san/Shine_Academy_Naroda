--------------------------------------------------

Definition

Revenue is the income earned by a business from its normal operating activities such as selling goods or providing services.

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

When a bakery sells bread,

the money earned is Revenue.

When a school collects tuition fees,

that is Revenue.

--------------------------------------------------

Examples

• Sales Revenue

• Service Revenue

• Commission Income

• Interest Income

• Rent Received

--------------------------------------------------

Importance

Revenue is the primary source of business income.

Higher revenue generally indicates increased business activity.

--------------------------------------------------

Difference

Revenue is not Profit.

Profit = Revenue – Expenses

--------------------------------------------------

Teacher Tip

Write on the board

Revenue ≠ Profit

Students often confuse these terms.

--------------------------------------------------

Common Mistakes

❌ Revenue means Profit.

✔ Profit is calculated after deducting expenses from revenue.

--------------------------------------------------

Learning Outcome

Students distinguish between Revenue and Profit.
