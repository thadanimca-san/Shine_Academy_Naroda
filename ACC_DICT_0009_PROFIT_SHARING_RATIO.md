Profit Sharing Ratio

Hindi
लाभ विभाजन अनुपात

Gujarati
નફા વહેંચણીનો ગુણોત્તર

Definition

Profit Sharing Ratio is the proportion in which partners divide the profits or losses of the business.

Simple Explanation

Suppose

A

B

C

agree to share profit in the ratio

3 : 2 : 1

If total profit is ₹60,000

A receives ₹30,000

B receives ₹20,000

C receives ₹10,000

Importance

Every partnership must decide how profits and losses will be shared.

Common Mistake

❌ Equal investment means equal profit.

✔ Profit sharing depends on the agreement.

Teacher Tip

Use chocolate bars to demonstrate sharing ratios.

Learning Outcome

Students calculate and interpret Profit Sharing Ratios.
