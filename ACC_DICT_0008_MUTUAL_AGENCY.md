Mutual Agency

Hindi
पारस्परिक अभिकरण

Gujarati
પરસ્પર પ્રતિનિધિત્વ

Definition

Mutual Agency means every partner is both a principal and an agent of the firm.

Simple Explanation

Suppose there are three partners.

A

B

C

Partner A purchases furniture for the business.

Even though B and C were absent,

the purchase binds the entire firm.

This happens because every partner represents the business.

Importance

Mutual Agency is considered the heart of Partnership.

Without Mutual Agency,

there is no Partnership.

Real Life Example

A law firm has four partners.

One partner signs an agreement with a client.

All partners become responsible.

Teacher Tip

Draw three stick figures on the board.

Use arrows showing that each partner can represent the others.

Learning Outcome

Students explain Mutual Agency using practical situations.
