Definition

A Transaction is any business event that has a financial effect and can be measured in monetary terms.

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

Buying goods

Selling goods

Paying salary

Receiving cash

Taking a loan

All these are transactions because they affect business finances.

--------------------------------------------------

Examples of Transactions

• Started business

• Purchased furniture

• Paid electricity bill

• Sold goods

• Received commission

• Paid wages

--------------------------------------------------

Not Transactions

• Appointment of manager

• Planning a meeting

• Signing an agreement without financial impact

--------------------------------------------------

Importance

Accounting records only financial transactions.

--------------------------------------------------

Teacher Tip

Ask students:

"Is celebrating the owner's birthday a business transaction?"

Discuss why or why not.

--------------------------------------------------

Learning Outcome

Students identify which events are accounting transactions.
