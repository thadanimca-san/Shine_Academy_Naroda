Definition

A Partner is a person who enters into a partnership agreement and shares the ownership, profit, responsibilities, and risks of the business.

Simple Explanation

A partner is not simply an investor.

A partner is one of the owners of the business.

Examples

Two doctors opening a clinic.

Three engineers starting a software company.

Two sisters opening a bakery.

Rights of a Partner

• Share Profit

• Participate in Management

• Inspect Books

• Receive Agreed Salary

• Receive Interest on Capital (if agreed)

Duties

• Work honestly

• Protect business interests

• Follow partnership agreement

• Maintain confidentiality

Liabilities

Partners are generally jointly responsible for business obligations according to the applicable legal structure.

Teacher Tip

Ask students to imagine starting a tuition class with a friend.

Immediately discuss responsibilities.
