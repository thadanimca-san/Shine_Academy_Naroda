Definition

A Ledger is the principal book in which transactions from the Journal are classified into individual accounts.

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

Journal records transactions date-wise.

Ledger groups transactions account-wise.

--------------------------------------------------

Example

Cash Account

Capital Account

Furniture Account

Sales Account

Purchase Account

Each account has its own page in the Ledger.

--------------------------------------------------

Importance

• Helps prepare Trial Balance

• Shows account balances

• Makes financial statements possible

--------------------------------------------------

Teacher Tip

Journal is like collecting letters.

Ledger is like sorting them into separate folders.
