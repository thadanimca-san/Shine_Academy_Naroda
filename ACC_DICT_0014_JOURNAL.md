Definition

A Journal is the book of original entry in which every financial transaction is recorded in chronological order before posting to the Ledger.

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

Think of the Journal as a business diary.

Whenever a business transaction occurs, it is first written in the Journal.

--------------------------------------------------

Why is Journal Important?

• First record of every transaction

• Helps maintain chronological order

• Reduces mistakes

• Provides complete transaction history

--------------------------------------------------

Example

Business started with Cash ₹2,00,000

Journal Entry

Cash A/c Dr. ₹2,00,000

     To Capital A/c ₹2,00,000

--------------------------------------------------

Golden Rule

Journal records every transaction first.

--------------------------------------------------

Teacher Tip

Compare the Journal to writing daily events in a personal diary.
