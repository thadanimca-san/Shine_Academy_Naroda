Definition

A Trial Balance is a statement prepared to verify the arithmetic accuracy of ledger accounts by comparing the total Debit balances with the total Credit balances.

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

Imagine counting all the money in your wallet.

If the total matches your record,

everything is correct.

Similarly,

Trial Balance checks whether accounting records are mathematically balanced.

--------------------------------------------------

Purpose

• Detect arithmetic errors.

• Prepare Financial Statements.

• Verify Ledger Posting.

• Ensure Debit equals Credit.

--------------------------------------------------

Example

Cash

Debit ₹80,000

Capital

Credit ₹80,000

Trial Balance

Debit ₹80,000

Credit ₹80,000

Balanced

--------------------------------------------------

Important Note

A balanced Trial Balance does NOT guarantee that every mistake has been detected.

Some errors can still remain.

--------------------------------------------------

Teacher Tip

Use a weighing scale.

Equal weight on both sides represents Debit and Credit.

--------------------------------------------------

Common Mistakes

❌ Trial Balance proves all accounts are correct.

✔ It proves only arithmetic accuracy.
