Definition

The Accounting Equation expresses the relationship between Assets, Liabilities, and Capital.

--------------------------------------------------

Formula

Assets = Capital + Liabilities

--------------------------------------------------

Meaning

Everything owned by the business has been financed either by

Owner

or

Outsiders.

--------------------------------------------------

Example

Cash ₹1,00,000

Furniture ₹50,000

Total Assets ₹1,50,000

Capital ₹1,20,000

Bank Loan ₹30,000

Accounting Equation

Assets

₹1,50,000

=

Capital

₹1,20,000

+

Liabilities

₹30,000

--------------------------------------------------

Importance

The Accounting Equation is the foundation of Double Entry Accounting.

Every transaction affects at least two accounts.

--------------------------------------------------

Applications

Journal Entries

Ledger

Trial Balance

Balance Sheet

Financial Statements

--------------------------------------------------

Common Mistakes

❌ Assets always equal Capital.

✔ Assets equal Capital plus Liabilities.

--------------------------------------------------

Teacher Tip

Draw a weighing balance.

One side = Assets

Other side = Capital + Liabilities

Students understand quickly.

--------------------------------------------------

Practice Question

Business started with

Cash ₹4,00,000

Loan ₹2,00,000

Calculate Assets.

Answer

₹6,00,000
