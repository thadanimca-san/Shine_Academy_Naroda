Definition

A Balance Sheet is a financial statement that shows the assets, liabilities, and owner's equity of a business on a specific date.

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

Think of taking a photograph of a business.

The photograph shows

• What the business owns.

• What the business owes.

• What belongs to the owner.

That photograph is called the Balance Sheet.

--------------------------------------------------

Formula

Assets = Capital + Liabilities

Importance

• Helps banks approve loans.
