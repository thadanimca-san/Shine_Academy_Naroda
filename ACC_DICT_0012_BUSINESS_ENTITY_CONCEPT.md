Definition

The Business Entity Concept states that the business and its owner are treated as two separate accounting entities.

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

Although the owner owns the business, accounting treats both separately.

The owner's personal money and business money should never be mixed.

--------------------------------------------------

Real Life Example

Mr. Shah owns a grocery shop.

He withdraws ₹20,000 for his family vacation.

This is Drawings, not Business Expense.

--------------------------------------------------

Importance

This concept forms the foundation of modern accounting.

Without it,

Capital,

Drawings,

Profit,

Loss,

and Balance Sheet

cannot be properly prepared.

--------------------------------------------------

Accounting Effect

Owner introduces money

↓

Business receives Capital

Business owes Owner

--------------------------------------------------

Common Mistakes

❌ Business and Owner are the same.

✔ Legally they may be connected, but accounting treats them separately.

--------------------------------------------------

Teacher Tip

Ask students:

"If you use your father's ATM card, whose money is it?"

Then explain separation between owner and business.

--------------------------------------------------

Learning Outcome

Students understand why Capital is treated as Liability.
