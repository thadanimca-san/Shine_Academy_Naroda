# Definition
## Beginner
Drawings are cash, goods, or other assets withdrawn by the owner from the business for personal use. Suppose the owner takes ₹20,000 from the business to pay school fees. This is not a business expense. It is Drawings.

## Intermediate
Drawings represent the withdrawal of resources by the owner, leading to a decrease in the owner's Capital.

## Advanced
Journal Entry for Drawings:
Drawings A/c Dr.
  To Cash A/c

# Real Life Examples
- Cash Withdrawn for personal use
- Goods Taken Home
- Personal Electricity Bill Paid by Business
- Furniture Taken by Owner

# Importance
## Accounting
- Reduces Owner's Capital
- Affects the Balance Sheet directly
- Fundamental for Partnership Accounts (Interest on Drawings)

# Teacher Notes
## Misconception
Wrong: Drawings are expenses. Correct: Drawings are owner's withdrawals. Ask students: "If the owner eats chocolates from his own shop, is it Sales?" Students usually laugh. Then explain Drawings.
