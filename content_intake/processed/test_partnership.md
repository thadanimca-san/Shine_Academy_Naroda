# Definition
## Beginner
Partnership is when two or more people agree to run a business together, contribute resources, and share profits and losses according to an agreement.

## Intermediate
Partnership is a legal relationship between persons carrying on a lawful business with the object of earning profit and sharing it according to agreed terms.

## Advanced
Partnership is a contractual relationship governed by applicable partnership laws, where partners jointly own and manage a business while acting as agents of one another.
