# Definition
## Beginner
Liabilities are obligations or debts that a business must pay to outsiders in the future. If you borrow money from a bank, you must return it. That responsibility is called a Liability.

## Intermediate
Liabilities are classified into Current Liabilities and Long-term Liabilities based on when they need to be repaid.

## Advanced
Liabilities show how much the business owes to others. They are a core component of the Accounting Equation (Assets = Capital + Liabilities).

# Real Life Examples
- A shop owner borrows ₹8,00,000 from a bank. The loan becomes a liability until it is repaid.
- Bank Loan, Creditors, Outstanding Expenses, Bills Payable, Mortgage.
