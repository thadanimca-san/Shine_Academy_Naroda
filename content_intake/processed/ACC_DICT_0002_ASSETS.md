# Definition
## Beginner
Assets are resources owned or controlled by a business that are expected to provide future economic benefits.
Think of a business as a toolbox. Everything useful inside that toolbox is an asset (Cash, Furniture, Building, Computers, Stock, Vehicles).

## Intermediate
Assets include every valuable resource that helps a business earn money, whether tangible or intangible.

## Advanced
Assets represent the strength of a business. They are classified into Current Assets, Non-Current Assets, and Intangible Assets. (Accounting Equation: Assets = Capital + Liabilities).

# Real Life Examples
- A medical clinic owns: Cash ₹5,00,000, Building ₹45,00,000, Medical Equipment ₹18,00,000, Furniture ₹3,00,000, Computer ₹1,50,000. These are all assets because they help the clinic operate.

# Teacher Notes
## Misconception
Wrong: Assets always mean cash. Correct: Assets include every valuable resource. Ask students: "What assets are present in your classroom?" (Desk, Chair, Projector). Then explain businesses also own assets.
