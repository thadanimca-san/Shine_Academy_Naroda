Definition

Loss occurs when Expenses exceed Revenue during an accounting period.

--------------------------------------------------

Formula

Loss = Expenses – Revenue

--------------------------------------------------

Simple Explanation

Revenue = ₹5,00,000

Expenses = ₹6,50,000

Loss = ₹1,50,000

--------------------------------------------------

Reasons

Poor Sales

High Expenses

Theft

Natural Disaster

Wrong Pricing

Poor Management

--------------------------------------------------

Importance

Understanding losses helps businesses improve future decisions.

--------------------------------------------------

Teacher Tip

Every successful company has experienced losses at some stage.

The goal is to learn and improve.

--------------------------------------------------

Common Mistakes

❌ Loss always means business closure.

✔ Temporary losses are common in business.
