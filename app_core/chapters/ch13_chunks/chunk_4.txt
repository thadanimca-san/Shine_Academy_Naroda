operating activities. Add back non-cash expenses and provisions to the profit of

assets as cash outflow.

inflow and reduction in share capital and non-current liabilities as cash outflow.

equivalent.

give closing balance of cash and cash equivalent.

closing balance of cash and cash equivalent it shows possibilities of all correct
calculations. The closing balance of cash and cash equivalent in cash flow
