the solvancy status of the business enterprise. As compared to the provious long-term and short-term

help of comparative balance sheet.

also is ascertained with the help of the analysis of balance sheet.

liabilities, (iv) Current liabilities as well as (i) Non-current assets, (ii) Current assets are changed

(increase/decrease) in terms of rupee and percentange can be ascertained from comperative balance
sheets.
